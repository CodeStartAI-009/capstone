How to read a result

Low-risk prediction
The model's phishing score is below its decision threshold. This does not mean the site is safe.
Phishing-risk prediction (moderate)
Above the decision threshold but below the high-confidence level.
Phishing-risk prediction (high)
At or above the level where 95% of validation flags were phishing.

Known limitations

  • Only the registrable domain is analysed; subdomains, paths, page content and domain age are not.
  • Phishing on ordinary-looking or compromised domains is often missed.
  • Trained on popular sites' homepages, so small legitimate sites are flagged more often.

Details: docs/model_evaluation.md and docs/ml_error_analysis.md.
